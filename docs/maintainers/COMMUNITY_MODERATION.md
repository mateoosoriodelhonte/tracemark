# Community moderation

Moderation protects participants and the project's privacy-sensitive support channels. Apply the
[code of conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) consistently, proportionately, and with the least public
disclosure needed to resolve a concern.

## Initial assessment

Preserve only the relevant public record and avoid quoting harmful, private, or security-sensitive
material more broadly. Determine whether the report is a conduct concern, support request,
vulnerability, spam, or ordinary technical disagreement. Route security-sensitive matters through
the private-contact process rather than asking for details in public.

## Response options

Depending on context and impact, maintainers can clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful
content, lock a thread, reject a contribution, limit participation, or arrange a private discussion.
Explain the action at a level that protects affected people and does not amplify the harmful content.

Technical disagreement, evidence-based criticism, and respectful requests for clarification are not
conduct violations. Do not use moderation tools to suppress a good-faith bug, accessibility, or
privacy concern.

## Record and follow up

Maintain a minimal private note only when needed to apply a decision consistently. Record date,
surface, action, rationale, and any follow-up without retaining unnecessary personal data. Review
the outcome when new context appears, and update public guidance only when it improves future
participation without revealing private details.
