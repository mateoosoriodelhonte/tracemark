# Community conduct

TraceMark welcomes focused, respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. This project is small, privacy-sensitive, and maintained in public, so clear
technical disagreement and considerate communication are both important.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, code, and evidence without attacking the person presenting them.
- Assume honest intent while still verifying security, privacy, and correctness claims.
- Make feedback specific and actionable; distinguish blocking defects from preferences.
- Respect consent and privacy. Never post another person's research, URLs, credentials, logs, or
  identifying information without permission.
- Keep public reports sanitized and use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for
  vulnerabilities.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope without harassment, even when proposing an alternative.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation,
repeated disruption, and publication of private or security-sensitive information are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, or other project spaces.

## Reporting and response

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a sanitized issue titled **Community conduct contact request**
with only enough information to arrange a private conversation. Do not publicly identify affected
people or reproduce harmful content when that would increase harm. Security reports follow the
separate private-contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate, protect affected people,
and preserve evidence only as needed to address the report. Maintainers will avoid promising a
specific outcome before reviewing the available context.

By participating in TraceMark project spaces, contributors agree to follow these expectations.
